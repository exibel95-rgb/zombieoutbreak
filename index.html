Three ways to fight

Survive the horde alone, hold it back with friends, or take on another team.

Survive the clock

Every wave is a countdown. Hold out until it reaches zero while the horde flanks you, lunges at you and climbs the kerbs to reach you. Runners and spitters arrive from the second wave, and every fifth wave brings a boss.

  • Waves last from 45 to 100 seconds
  • Up to 24 zombies on you at once
  • Ten mutant types plus toad monsters

Fight side by side

Bring up to four friends into the same waves. One of you hosts on your home Wi-Fi and the others join by IP. Everyone keeps their own guns, levels and loadout.

  • Up to five survivors
  • Shared waves and bosses
  • Worldwide online rooms are on the way

Win it as a team

Team Deathmatch, Element Core, Boss Hunt and Horde Wars. Bots fill every empty slot, so a full match starts the moment you tap play.

  • Four modes, ten-minute matches
  • Ranked or classic
  • Every map is open for 5v5

Rise from Rookie to Legendary

Ranked 5v5 matches earn you Rank Points. Your place in the team, MVP and win streaks add more, and a strong game takes the sting out of a loss.

  1. Rookiefrom 0 RP

Tiers to climb
Rookie, Veteran, Elite and Pro each have five tiers, I to V. Master, Grandmaster and Legendary are earned as one.
Promotion shield
Every new tier shields you from your next loss, and you never fall out of a rank once you reach it.
Ranked series
A new series starts every two months. Your rank steps back and you are paid in Parts for how high you climbed.
Bots at your level
Ranked bots carry ranks close to yours and sharpen their aim as you climb.

Start playing

Play straight away in your browser, or install the Android client for full speed on your phone.

In your browser

Nothing to install. Best on a computer.

  • Chrome, Edge or Firefox on Windows, Mac, Linux or a Chromebook
  • Move with WASD, aim with the mouse and fire with the left button. R reloads, Q swaps guns, E opens the element box, V changes the camera and Esc frees the mouse.
  • The first start downloads about 360 MB. Your browser keeps it, so the next start is quick.
  • Your progress is saved in the browser.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There is nothing to buy. Parts, the in-game currency, are earned by playing.

Do I need an account?

No. Pick a survivor name and play. Progress is saved on your phone, or in your browser for the web version.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. On the same Wi-Fi, one player hosts and the others join by IP. Worldwide online rooms are being set up. Until they are live, 5v5 matches fill the empty slots with bots.

Android warns about an unknown app. Is that normal?

Yes. The game is not on Google Play, so Android asks once whether your browser may install apps. Every release is signed with the same key, so updates install over the old version.

Will an update wipe my progress?

No. A new version installs over the old one and keeps your guns, levels and rank.
